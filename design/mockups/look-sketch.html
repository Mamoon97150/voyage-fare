What I’d watch for

  • Amounts stay in the clean font in all three: hand-drawn numbers are harder to trust, and trust is the product.
  • Busy screens (Compare, Who paid what) carry a lot of information: the more drawing, the harder they get to read. B keeps drawings for welcome and empty pages.
  • These drawings are mine, quick sketches to show the idea. For the real app, the illustrations would be made properly in one consistent style (drawn or generated, then cleaned up as vector line art).
  • You can mix: e.g. B’s drawings with C’s hand-written titles.
