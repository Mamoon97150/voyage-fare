Decided by Djamina (13 Nov)

  • Main currency fixed once a price exists (every saved rate is “trip currency per unit”). Changing it later would mean re-entering rates. ✅ Revised: locked at the first payment; before that, changing it converts every saved rate.
  • Removing a traveller who paid or owes: ✅ blocked until settled up, rather than rewriting who-owes-whom silently.
  • ✅ Invite = link to a traveller. The invited person picks nothing: the organizer says who they are, so their “your share” is right from the start.
  • Delete only by the person who created the trip; others can leave. ✅ If the creator deletes their account, another member takes over and can delete it.
