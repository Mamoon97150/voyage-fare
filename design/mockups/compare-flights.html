Decided by Djamina (13 Nov)

  • ✅ Journeys instead of places on the Travel tab, in route order.
  • ✅ Two one-way tickets can be one option (Alizé's recommendation, from St Martin it's often the cheapest): a price per ticket, added up; one choice, booked and paid as one.
  • ✅ Durations worked out from the times, for now. Arrival the next day shows “+1”. Known limit: times are local, so a time difference (St Martin UTC−4, Colombia UTC−5) makes the duration off by an hour — airport time zones later.
  • ✅ Fastest = shortest total, way out + way back. A tag and a sort.
  • ✅ Airport on each leg (SXM Princess Juliana or SFG Grand Case…) and refundable yes/no.
  • ✅ Train, bus, ferry: the same card; stops as a number (direct, 1, 2…); no bag field (luggage is almost always included).
